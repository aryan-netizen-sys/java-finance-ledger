# Problem Statement
Many individuals struggle to track their daily expenses and manage their budgets effectively without relying on complex, resource-heavy software[cite: 1]. This project provides a lightweight, command-line-based personal finance tracker that allows users to quickly log income and expenses, securely store them locally, and generate analytical reports to understand their spending habits[cite: 1].

# Scope of the Project
The project is confined to a local command-line environment[cite: 1]. It will handle user authentication, basic CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) operations for financial transactions, and file-based data persistence[cite: 1]. It will not require external databases or internet connectivity.

# Target Users
* Students managing their monthly allowances[cite: 1].
* Freelancers tracking project-based income and expenses[cite: 1].
* Anyone who prefers terminal-based, fast, and local financial tracking[cite: 1].

# High-Level Features
1. **User Authentication:** Simple local PIN login to protect financial data[cite: 1].
2. **Transaction Management:** Add, delete, and view income/expense records[cite: 1].
3. **Data Persistence:** Save and load financial data from a local CSV file[cite: 1].
4. **Analytics & Reporting:** Generate summaries of total income, total expenses, and current balance[cite: 1].
